# Prototype A — Eggventure

A 2D platformer where you play an egg: roll and float as an egg, grow legs in a warm spot, dash to smash rocks (every impact cracks your shell), and hatch into a bird on the third crack to fly home to the nest.

- 4 levels (The Big Tree, The Creek, Storm Peak, The Frozen Hollow), one life per try; finishing a level unlocks the next.
- Record time per level: level time + 10 s for every seed left behind.
- Keyboard: ← → move · SPACE jump · SHIFT dash · hold ↓ tuck · hold R restart level · P pause · M mute.
- Phones: touch controls turn on automatically (toggle with the corner button). Add it to your home screen to play it as an app.

Everything is in `index.html` (no build step). `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` and `icons/` make it installable.
